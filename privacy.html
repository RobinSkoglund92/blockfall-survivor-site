Blockfall Survivor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Blockfall Survivor is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Robin Skoglund, a sole trader in Sweden. This policy explains what information is used when you play, who handles it and what choices you have.

The short version

  • The developer runs no servers and collects no personal information about you.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and, if you use Game Center and iCloud, in your own iCloud account.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use information about your device to show and measure ads, as described below. You can say no to personalised ads and to tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.

Information the game keeps

The game saves your progress, such as scores, unlocked items, coins, settings and achievements, in a file on your device. If you are signed in to Game Center and iCloud, the game also stores a copy in Game Center's saved games in your iCloud account, so your progress follows you to your other devices. This data stays with Apple and your device; the developer cannot see it.

Apple: Game Center, iCloud and purchases

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center to show leaderboards and achievements. The optional Remove Ads purchase is made through the App Store; Apple handles the payment and tells the game only whether you own the purchase. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob: advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch to double your coins, and ads that may play between rounds. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device information, such as the device model, operating system version and language;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the game asks;
  • information about the ads shown and your interactions with them;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads library.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game first asks for your consent through Google's consent form. On iOS, Apple also asks whether you allow the game to track you. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised and the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with Privacy in the game's main menu, and the tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google is responsible for this processing. Read more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and delete personal information about you and to object to its use. Since the developer holds no personal information about you, requests about ads should go to Google and requests about Game Center, iCloud and purchases to Apple, through the links above. Deleting the game removes its local save; progress in iCloud can be removed under Settings > [your name] > iCloud. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in Sweden the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If the game starts using information in new ways, this policy will be updated and the date at the top changed.
